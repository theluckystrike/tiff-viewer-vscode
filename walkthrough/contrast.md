# Channel and contrast

8-bit images open as stored. 16-bit, 32-bit and float images open with a percentile stretch (0.5 to 99.5), so a dim 16-bit image does not look black.

- View picks the RGB composite or one channel. Planar files with several channels work the same way.
- Stretch offers percentile 0.5 to 99.5, percentile 2 to 98, min to max, and the full type range.
- The Values section shows min, max, mean, nodata and NaN counts, and a histogram with the shown range marked.
- Zoom with the wheel, drag to pan, double click to switch between fit and 1:1. Keys: 0 fit, 1 actual pixels, + and -.
